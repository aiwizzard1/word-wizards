# Word Wizards

Interactive grammar and spelling practice for 4th graders: spelling rules, parts of speech, subject and predicate, sentence diagramming (including linking verbs), sentence skills, and word parts.

Single-file app: open `index.html` in any browser. Progress is saved in the browser only; no data is collected.

Content follows the Core Knowledge Sequence Grade 4 grammar and morphology, and Riggs spelling rules 1-21.

- Diagramming and grammar topics also follow the scope of Well-Ordered Language Level 3A (Classical Academic Press): four sentence kinds, principal elements, adjectives and adverbs, predicate nominatives and adjectives, prepositional phrases, personal and interrogative pronouns, sensory linking verbs, indirect objects, relative clauses. Diagram levels 7-8 (indirect objects, mixed review) and the Grammar Workbook Review quiz come from this list. Only the published topic list was available online, so wording and diagram style may differ from the workbook. Relative clauses are quiz-only (not diagrammed). Diagram level 9 adds compound subjects and objects (dotted "spaceship" with the conjunction), understood (You) in commands, and phrases hanging under any word. "Mark the Sentence" mirrors the workbook's analysis marking (S, hv, PV, do, lv, PN, PA, p, op, understood (You) for commands) and its oral analysis steps, based on photographed pages from Chapters 1-4.
